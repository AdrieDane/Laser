combinations lose 15 correct matches: 81,896 -> 81,881 of 90,871 manual entries

the total. They remain in every full report, but are not the current focus.

90%. Fewer extras do not justify more wrong peaks and worse common-class results.

What was tested

Known, independently detected spikes estimate an exact-class, same-stream RT
offset against isotope-specific transfer references learned in other projects.
Transfer-only spike detections cannot feed back as anchors. Single-chemical
support needs at least 10 transfer groups and MAD <= 2 seconds; multiple
chemicals need at least 3 groups each, MAD <= 3 seconds and offsets agreeing
within 3 seconds. Maximum absolute shift is 30 seconds; supported atlas domain
and uncertainty gates remain binding. No ether/lyso class merging was added.

All old 029 physical candidates and original coordinates were retained. New
proposals use original features at raw absolute mass error < 3 ppm and RT error
<= 10 seconds from the supported aligned prediction. Declared spike masses and
measured spike feature IDs are protected. Native chemical targets and the
existing group-excluded composition envelope are unchanged; no rare-catalogue
expansion, wider mass/RT window, new matrix limiter or formula guess was used.
There were 766 additional physical candidate rows across the 33 outer test
pools; 7,630 across all 363 reference masks. These are NOT accepted annotations
or correct-match gains. Competition evidence was recomputed on expanded pools.

Eight numerical aligned-RT descriptors were added to old and new candidates,
alongside the existing 15 exact standard-context fields. All four mode experts
were freshly fitted: 100 classifiers, 180 rounds each, in 25 nested parents.
Old labelled pairs were preserved. New labels enter only AFTER label-free
generation, for permitted training/calibration groups. No query/outer/inner
group enters its learned references. The prior training-only operating-point
objective, 9.5% endogenous-manual extras budget, 0.05 ambiguity margin, 005
standard cleanup and fixed 027 mass stage were retained. No rejected 030
partial labels, 031 related-standard descriptors or 032 adduct descriptors.
