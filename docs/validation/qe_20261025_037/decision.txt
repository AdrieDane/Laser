What changed
Only training weights: a negative example received quarter weight when its
class was absent from this training dataset's manual mode, but present in
another manual mode, provided it contradicted neither a curated identity nor
a mapped curated peak. All original labels, candidates and predictors stayed
unchanged. Project totals were equalized within each mode. All four mode
classifiers were freshly fitted at 180 rounds: 25 parents, 100 classifiers.
The original 009 calibration, 005 standard handling and fixed 027 mass stage
were retained. No rejected experiment's class/mode winners were spliced in.

Interpretation
Several weak classes improved: BMP gained 86 correct matches in NP negative
and 81 in NP positive. But their extras rose by 177 and 64 respectively.
There were material losses elsewhere, including NP-positive PC (-77),
RP-positive PC (-34), DG (-32), TG-O (-28), DG-O (-24) and TG (-22).
RP-positive PE fell from 1953/2209 (88.4%) to 1943/2209 (88.0%).
These gains do not outweigh the overall correct-match loss and extra calls.
The predeclared total-correct and extras guards fail, so the WHOLE experiment
is rejected. Its improved individual classes are not adopted separately.

Limitations and next step
These are grouped held-out saved-input research replays, NOT fresh end-to-end
production runs or untouched external validation. Prior development exposure
and inherited automatic/vendor references remain. Manual-absent calls are
unconfirmed extras, not established false positives or an analytical FDR.
