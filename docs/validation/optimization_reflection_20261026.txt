LASER optimization reflection — pause after experiment 049
26 October 2026

Bottom line

There has been substantial cumulative improvement, but recent cycles have
largely plateaued. The best retained research method is still experiment 041.
Experiment 049 is fully checked and rejected. No new cycle starts at this pause.
The requested 90% in EACH curated class/mode is not achieved. The current
installed app has NOT been upgraded to use these research models by default.

Scope and definitions

The sole active collection has 33 distinct four-peak-list sets /132 mode cases,
with related acquisitions grouped into 30 project groups. User-requested
exclusions remain excluded; archived datasets have not been reintroduced.
Correct = same lipid, adduct, measurement mode AND ORIGINAL feature as manual.
Primary denominator 92,100 includes standards and 28 unresolved manual mappings;
only X4 (two manual entries) is user-excluded. Sparse classes are deferred for
dedicated tuning, NOT dropped from evaluation. Extras are TOTAL calls absent
from the corresponding manual list, not proven false chemical identities/FDR.
Percentages pool counts first; they are not averages of dataset percentages.

2. What has been implemented and kept in the research method?

- Learned class/composition RT references and chemical target/range coverage,
  alongside existing reference/automatic RT information, rather than blanket
  conversion of candidate labels into confident annotations.
- Combined scoring of RT, mass error, isotopes, composition/competition and
  run context; strongest-feature and competing-label selection safeguards.
- Four separately fitted measurement-mode experts with project-balanced
  training and training-only acceptance calibration.
- Query/outer/inner-project exclusions in learned references and predictors;
  related acquisition folders are not treated as independent validation sets.
- Dedicated declared-internal-standard handling and independently measured
  standard context. Conservative fixed mass-calibrated discovery is retained.
- A small, guarded completion stage for free, clear class-specific matches.
  Its measured 029->041 contribution is only 11 correct matches and two extras.
- Reproducible all-dataset reporting, same-original-feature comparisons,
  separate extras/wrong peaks/unresolved mappings, source/input binding,
  independently reconstructed selections/calibrations and original-data checks.

These are implemented research components and saved validated experiments,
NOT an assertion that the installed app currently runs the retained pipeline.
The final integrated-app benchmark and requested deployment exports remain due.

3. What have we tested but NOT kept?

The repository contains many opt-in prototypes/rejected experiments. Their
existence is not evidence that all their changes are active in the best method.
Tested families include class/family weighting and specialists; alternative
negative-label treatment; more/redistributed acceptance cutoffs; pairwise peak
and same-feature isomer-label models; additional RT/mass/standard discovery;
raw cross-polarity, adduct-family and sample-profile evidence; BMP-specific
completion strategies; and a different machine-learning family (CatBoost).
Read-only pilots also examined isotope-formula compatibility, competing-ion
families, occupancy, paired RP isomer RT gaps and sample-context priors.
Read-only physical candidate opportunities are NOT accepted annotation gains.

Examples of why rejection matters:
- 047 BMP completion gained 391 correct but added 459 extras and three wrong
  peaks. It exceeded its prospectively stated trade-off: not retained.
- 048 alternative learner/calibration lost 1,706 correct and added 18 extras,
  despite 42 fewer wrong peaks. Not retained; favorable modes were not spliced.
- 049 sample context lost 25 correct and added 17 wrong peaks while reducing
  extras by 27. Not retained. All five calibration and all 33 output checks pass;
  correct implementation does not imply better annotation performance.

The 20 numbered experiments after 029 (030 through 049) produced only ONE
retained improvement: 041, +11 correct /+2 extras /no wrong-peak change.
That is +0.01194354 percentage points, NOT a large recent accuracy improvement.
Most cumulative gains came earlier. The recent pace has not been adequate.

4. What is still missing?

Retained 041 has 43/86 well-represented class/mode combinations below 90%, and
76/122 overall below 90%. The well-represented combinations pool to 90.14%,
but that pooled result is NOT the requested 90% in each combination.

Examples, current retained method (standards included in each class scope):
PE RPpos 1,953/2,209=88.41%; RPneg2,461/2,621=93.90%;
PE NPpos 1,973/2,158=91.43%; NPneg2,036/2,251=90.45%.
BMP NPpos270/622=43.41%; NPneg146/520=28.08%.
FA-OH NPneg152/293=51.88%; RPneg350/502=69.72%.
RPnegative LPI223/261=85.44% and 2-acylLPE378/448=84.38% need better peak choice
as well as discovery. BMP must remain allowed in both NP polarities when
supported; opposite-polarity manual support does not exempt an extra call.

The completed 029 candidate audit (historical snapshot, not a new 041 audit)
found 6,215 missing manual matches with a correct candidate already available,
3,128 mapped missing matches without one, and 28 unresolved mappings. Even
optimistic row-wise candidate availability could not reach 90% for 31/122
class/modes; it ignores competing assignments and is not attainable accuracy.
The 041 gain is too small to dismiss that fundamental discovery/selection gap.

Locking every existing call also has structural limits: even perfect free
discovery could not bring RPnegative 2-acylLPE or LPI to 90% in the independent
immutable-call audit. Some original peak choices eventually must change safely.
More permissive cutoffs alone cannot create an absent correct candidate or
reliably separate same-formula isomers. Mass/profile agreement alone was not
a consistently reliable original-peak discriminator in the completed pilots.

5. My assessment and recommendation for resuming

We have a materially improved, well-instrumented RESEARCH prototype and a much
better picture of its failure modes. We do NOT have a demonstrated deployable
solution to the class/mode target. More implementation and more audit code
should not be presented as more measured annotation accuracy.

The next phase should prioritize a materially different class-specific physical
strategy for the dominant gaps, rather than more near-equivalent classifier or
cutoff variants. In particular, evaluate joint RT relationships across a lipid
series and competing original features, with separate RP isomer treatment and
NP class behavior. New discovery and safe correction of existing peak choices
must both be allowed; retaining all calls forever cannot meet the target.
This is a proposed direction, NOT implemented/tested here or a promised gain.
Review existing local-plane/constant-shift/paired-gap work first to avoid
repackaging rejected methods. Start with a prospectively defined numeric pilot
that can distinguish true candidates from rivals, then a fully grouped actual
experiment with the same full reporting and explicit extras/wrong-peak guards.

The validation process itself also needs more efficiency: reuse unchanged,
hash-verified physical constructions and controls where scientifically valid,
while keeping genuinely new model/calibration/output checks complete. Do not
repeat unchanged work simply to add another cycle or weaken independent checks
to make an experiment look successful. Never select outer-tested class winners
from rejected runs as a disguised deployment ensemble.

Repeated optimization on these 33 datasets creates development-selection bias.
Grouped checks reduce direct leakage, but these are not untouched external
data and inherited/vendor exposure remains. Future independent acquisitions
would be useful when available; no unavailable new collection is substituted
for the current objective. An extra call is unconfirmed, not automatically
chemically wrong; agreement against curation is not analytical FDR.

Before deployment, integrate a defensible retained method into the actual app,
run fresh end-to-end original-input annotations on all 33 datasets, verify
peakgroup exports add ONLY columns 2/3 laser_annotation/adduct with original
cells/rows intact, and produce the requested single colleague-ready report.
None of that is claimed complete at this pause. No deployment-ready exports
are generated from the rejected 049 experiment.
