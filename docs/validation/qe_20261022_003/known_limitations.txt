Known standard-protection limitation in experiments 003 and 004

The saved endogenous chemical reference omits internal-standard rows. The
compiler's embedded-reference mass-identical-spike check therefore had no
complete declaration catalog in these two experiments. Previously accepted
standard FEATURES were still reserved, and all standard/manual flags were
scored correctly; the reported correct/extra/wrong-feature counts describe the
actual outputs and remain unchanged. Neither experiment is deployed.

The current development compiler accepts an explicit declared-standard catalog
and flags missing protection input in its audit. A separate pure chemical
compiler now produces declared targets even for standards not detected earlier.
Recovery/standard-aware adjudication requires a new, fully reported experiment;
do not silently rewrite the frozen experiment003/004 candidates or scores.
