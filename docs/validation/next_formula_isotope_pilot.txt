Native formula/isotope compatibility: prospective read-only measurement pilot

Not annotation experiment 048. No annotations, classifier fits, learned cuts,
app defaults, candidate coordinates, original intensities or exports change.
Sole active 33 QE datasets; all four measurement streams. X4 remains excluded
only from evaluation, standards remain in all original benchmark denominators.

Question: do formula-aware descriptions of existing raw isotope-neighbour
intensity ratios provide usable correct-original-peak discrimination beyond
the generic raw evidence already present? PE already has a rough carbon-only
envelope descriptor; this pilot must not claim isotope evidence is new.

For each single outer-excluded candidate pool, derive an ion atom formula from
the BUNDLED native catalogue chemistry/R-group increments and the actual native
phase-specific adduct. Match reported species and class; NP O+P-d corresponds
to O-d / P-(d-1). Require a UNIQUE atom formula and agreement with native target
m/z within 0.00002 Da. Never infer chemistry from a mass alone or query manual
labels. Unsupported/labelled/spiked/ambiguous formula rows remain UNKNOWN.
This is a formula-description eligibility check, not a new mass matching gate.
Same-formula isomers MUST receive identical theoretical isotope predictions.

Use bundled stable_isotopes.tsv abundances; ion mass accounts for signed charge
and electron mass. Compute carbon-only M1/M2 ratios and nominal full-element
M1/M2 totals analytically. Existing raw features searched C13 upper/lower and
lower2 at charge-aware spacing, NOT upper2. Fine isotope mass components and
instrument resolving power are unknown: nominal totals are descriptive upper
compatibility bounds, NOT measured envelopes. Do not claim Cl37/S34 was seen
at a C13-squared location. No hard ratio/profile gate is introduced.

Invert existing log1p intensity differences using the original log1p median to
obtain exact positive median ratios. Zero, absent or invalid intensities remain
NaN. Descriptive log distance to carbon-only prediction and carbon-to-nominal
intervals for upper and inverse lower/lower2 ratios. Lower matches describe
possible isotope contamination, not definitive parent/identity assignments.
All 14 fields frozen for ALL33 before any manual-label analysis.

Independent checker: separate raw TSV catalogue parser/formula construction,
native affine mass arithmetic, isotope polynomial convolution rather than the
producer's analytic moments, and direct expm1 intensity ratio inversion. Check
all rows/formulas/fields/unknowns, input and output hashes, original-pool
immutability and source outer-exclusion provenance. Synthetic tests include
12 native adducts, multiply charged targets, NP O/P equivalence, standards,
missing/zero channels, low intensity log1p inversion and unsupported formulas.

Only AFTER all33 blind outputs and all33 independent checks pass, descriptive
manual analysis may report per-dataset/mode/class measurement coverage and
within-identity correct-peak versus known-wrong-peak contrasts. Predeclared
contrast fields: upper_carbon_error and upper_interval_error, lower is better;
lower_inverse_interval_error and lower2_inverse_interval_error, larger is
less isotope-like (not required for correct annotations). Require both true
and alternative wrong candidates measurable; compare true to the BEST wrong
value in that direction, report ties and project counts. Exactly one mapped
manual feature per identity; deduplicate candidate-construction versions.
Both true and wrong options must pass the pre-existing independent physical
3ppm/10second/standard/isotope protections for that query-excluded pool. Report
current-correct and currently-missing comparisons separately, not just winners.
Also report formula-coverage/unknown statuses without labels. No per-class
cutoff selection, accepted-gain claims or promotion based on these exposed
development data. Any later annotation method needs a new prospective plan,
full nested masked training and the mandatory full132 before/after report.
